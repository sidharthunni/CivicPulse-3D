## 1. Executive Summary

Indian cities face an urgent municipal crisis: urban flash floods, collapsing roadways, drainage choke points, and unmonitored civic hazards. Current governance systems rely on flat, 2D text forms and siloed departments where citizens submit complaints that disappear into black holes while departments engage in jurisdictional blame games.

**CivicPulse 3D** transforms urban governance by introducing **India's First 3D Urban Spatial Digital Twin** coupled with an **Autonomous Multi-Agency Routing Engine**. It enables municipal authorities to visualize cities in full 3D geospatial reality, simulate flood inundation and slope hazards in real time, and empower citizens with a zero-friction, ultra-accessible reporting portal with cryptographic public resolution tracking.

## 3. Core Modules & Architecture

1. **3D Urban Spatial Digital Twin (Command Center)**:
   - High-fidelity WebGL / Three.js spatial view of urban infrastructure.
   - Real-time heatmaps for structural road fatigue, drainage chokes, and electrical hazards.
   - Dynamic simulation controls: Monsoon Inundation Slider, Slope Vulnerability Index, and Evacuation Routing.

2. **Ultra-Accessible Citizen Portal**:
   - Zero-installation mobile interface designed for citizens across all literacy levels.
   - One-click geo-hazard pinning with automatic GPS reverse-geocoding.
   - Voice-to-text reporting supporting English, Malayalam, and Hindi.

3. **Autonomous Multi-Agency Dispatch Agent**:
   - Analyzes reported incident metadata, GPS proximity, and severity to automatically assign work orders to relevant municipal departments (PWD, Water Authority, Electricity Board, Fire & Rescue).
   - Detects interdependent infrastructure conflicts (e.g., prevents road surfacing before pipeline replacement).

4. **Public Accountability & Audit Ledger**:
   - Transparent tracking of every municipal ticket with time-stamped status transitions: Reported, Verified, Dispatched, In-Progress, and Publicly Verified.
